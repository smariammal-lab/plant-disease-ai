🌿 Supported Tomato Diseases

🦠 Bacterial Spot

Causes dark spots on tomato leaves and fruits.

🍂 Early Blight

Creates dark circular lesions and yellowing of older leaves.

🌧️ Late Blight

Causes dark, water-soaked lesions and rapid leaf damage.

🍃 Leaf Mold

Often appears as yellow patches with mold growth underneath leaves.

💚 Healthy

The leaf shows no detected signs of the supported diseases.

💻 About This Project

Plant Disease Detection AI is an AI-powered system designed to identify common tomato leaf diseases from uploaded images. The system uses a trained deep learning model to analyze the leaf image and provide a predicted disease with a confidence score and basic advisory.
